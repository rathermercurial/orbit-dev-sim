The Design team crafts the visual language and interaction patterns that define every product experience. James and David balance aesthetic ambition with rigorous accessibility standards, building systems that scale gracefully across devices and assistive technologies. They steward the Nebula design system, from foundational tokens to complex component behaviors. Every pattern is tested for color contrast, keyboard flow, and screen reader clarity before it enters production, ensuring beauty never comes at the cost of inclusion.
