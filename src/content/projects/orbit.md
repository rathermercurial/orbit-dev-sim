Orbit is the central orchestration layer that coordinates data pipelines, deployment workflows, and service discovery across our distributed infrastructure. It provides a declarative configuration model and a real-time control plane that operators use to manage fleet health and rolling updates. Alice designed the core scheduling engine and failover logic that keeps services resilient during peak load, while Bob crafted the operator dashboard and visual status indicators that make complex systems readable at a glance.
