Nebula is the design system that unifies typography, color, spacing, and motion across every product surface in our ecosystem. It ships as a comprehensive token library and a React component catalog with built-in accessibility testing and visual regression checks. Carol shaped the governance model and contribution guidelines that keep the system healthy as it grows, while Eve led the accessibility audit and ensured every component meets WCAG 2.1 AA standards before it reaches production.
