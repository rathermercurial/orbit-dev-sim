Solar is a monitoring dashboard that aggregates metrics, logs, and traces into unified views for platform operators and engineering leads. It supports custom alerts, anomaly detection, and historical trend analysis that help teams respond to incidents before customers ever notice a degradation. David designed the information hierarchy and responsive layout system that surfaces the right signal without noise, while James defined the alert taxonomy and escalation matrices that keep on-call rotations humane and effective.
