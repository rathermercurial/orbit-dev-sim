Stellar is a content distribution network that delivers documentation, release notes, and design assets to edge locations around the world. It uses intelligent caching and incremental updates to ensure users always see the latest version without redundant downloads or stale artifacts. David designed the reader experience and navigation patterns that make long-form content scannable, while Elena built the asset pipeline and visual theming engine that adapts content to different device contexts and accessibility preferences.
