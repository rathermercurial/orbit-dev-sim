Welcome to Orbit. After eighteen months of quiet development, we are opening the doors to the developer community and sharing what we have been building behind the scenes.

Orbit began as a simple question: what if infrastructure observability felt as intuitive as reading a well-designed dashboard? Our team started with satellite telemetry pipelines, but the patterns we discovered turned out to be universal. Whether you are managing a microservices fleet or a single-page application, the same principles apply. You need clear signals, humane alerts, and the ability to trace a symptom back to its cause without clicking through twenty tabs.

Today we are releasing the first public version of our platform, complete with the Aurora data pipeline, the Solar monitoring dashboard, and the Cosmos documentation library. Every component has been battle-tested internally across dozens of releases and thousands of operational hours.

Over the coming months we will publish deep dives into architecture decisions, design constraints, and the lessons we learned while scaling. We will also share our roadmap and invite contributions from teams who want to push the boundaries of developer experience.

Thank you for being here at the beginning. The best way to get started is to read the Quick Start guide, clone the examples repository, and join the community forum. We cannot wait to see what you build.
