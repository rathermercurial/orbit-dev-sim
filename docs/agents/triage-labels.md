# Triage label vocabulary

- `needs-triage` — freshly filed; Hermes investigates and proposes scope.
- `needs-info` — missing details a human must provide before scoping.
- `ready-for-agent` — human-approved scope; dispatch to a worktree agent.
- `ready-for-human` — work a human should do (e.g. merge).
- `wontfix` — out of scope; closed with a reason.
